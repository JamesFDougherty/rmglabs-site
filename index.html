About

Veteran. Infrastructure engineer. Now building apps.

I am James Dougherty, a US Army veteran with 29 years in IT. I started as a Windows administrator, supported web development for several years, and have spent the last 27 years supporting large Fortune 500 VMware environments, working across storage, databases and networking along the way. Lately that includes Red Hat OpenShift clusters.

All of that time I wrote PowerShell, PowerCLI, VBScript and JavaScript to streamline the daily work of big teams. That habit of finding the tedious thing and building a tool for it is what led me to phone apps.

Small businesses deserve the same quality big companies get. A local gym, a coffee roaster or a service shop can have an app that is reliable, secure and simple for customers to use, built with an operations mindset: it has to work every day, and it has to protect people's information.
