Beam

Early prototype

One phone remote for every TV in the house. Beam finds the TVs on your Wi-Fi and controls Roku, Samsung, Google TV and Fire TV sets from a single app, so you stop hunting for the right remote.

What it does

  • Works with Roku, Samsung (including older models), Google TV and Fire TV
  • Full remote: directional pad, playback, volume, channels and one-tap input switching (TV, HDMI 1 to 4)
  • Saved TV list with a live status for each set, such as "Ready to control"
  • Scans your Wi-Fi to find TVs, or add one by typing its address
  • Rename your TVs and keep one shared list across every phone in the house
  • Pairing and setup tips right on the screen for each TV brand

Beam is an early prototype, still being tested on more TV models. It is not in the app stores yet.

Try the live demo

The demo runs in your browser with sample TVs. It does not connect to anything real.
