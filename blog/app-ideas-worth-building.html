Four App Ideas Worth Building

By RMG LabsNovember 18, 20264 min read

Good apps usually start as a small annoyance someone got tired of. Here are four ideas in three realms: small business, IT administration and everyday life. To be clear, these are ideas only. None of them has been built, and nothing below describes a product you can download today.

Idea 1: A Walk-In Waitlist for Small Shops

Who it helps: barbers, salons, repair shops and small restaurants that take walk-ins and manage the line on a clipboard.

Core features: customers join the queue from a QR code at the door, see their place in line, and get a text or push notification when they are nearly up. The owner gets a simple list to reorder, skip or remove people.

Why it is useful: customers can wait somewhere comfortable instead of standing in the doorway, and the business stops losing people who walk out because they could not tell how long the wait would be.

Idea 2: A Read-Only VMware Snapshot Watcher

Who it helps: small IT teams running vSphere who occasionally discover a snapshot that has been quietly growing for months.

Core features: a scheduled read-only job lists snapshots by age, size and creator, and a mobile-friendly page shows the oldest ones first. It would send a gentle reminder to the owner of each stale snapshot. It would never delete anything on its own.

Why it is useful: forgotten snapshots eat datastore space and can slow virtual machines down. A steady, low-effort reminder is easier to live with than an occasional cleanup scramble.

Idea 3: A Change-Window Checklist Companion

Who it helps: admins who run patching or maintenance windows, often late in the evening, often from memory.

Core features: reusable checklists for pre-checks, the change itself and post-checks. Each step can be ticked off, timestamped and annotated, and a short summary can be shared with the team afterwards. Steps could link to the approved scripts your team already uses.

Why it is useful: it turns tribal knowledge into a repeatable routine, makes handoffs easier and leaves a simple record of what happened during the window.

Idea 4: A Household Warranty Vault

Who it helps: anyone who has ever hunted for a receipt after an appliance died.

Core features: snap a photo of the receipt and the model plate, enter the warranty length, and get a reminder before coverage ends. Everything is searchable by item or store, and the data can be exported at any time.

Why it is useful: it solves a small but real problem, and it works equally well for a family or a tiny business tracking equipment.

How Ideas Become Apps

Each idea above is small enough to start as a simple first version. RMG Labs builds apps this way, as with The Coffee Shop and Beam: begin with one job done well, then grow from real feedback.
