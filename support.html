Support

Need help with one of our apps? Email James@RMGLabs.net and include the app name, your device and OS version, and a short description of the problem. We aim to reply within a few business days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I report a bug?

Email us with the steps that caused it and, if you can, a screenshot.

How do I request a feature?

Send us your idea. We read every message.

How do I delete my data?

Email us from the address tied to your account and we will remove your data. See our Privacy Policy for details.
