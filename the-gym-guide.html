Welcome to The GYM app

The GYM app is the all-in-one app for a local training gym. Members use it to join, see gym hours and their membership, and track workouts. Trainers use it to manage clients and log training sessions. Owners and admins use it to approve new members, record payments and run the gym.

Who this guide is for

If you are... Start here What you can do
A member Part 1 Join, check hours and your membership, see your workout history, call the gym
A trainer Part 2 See your clients, run and log training sessions, manage exercises, get help in an emergency
An owner or admin Part 3 Approve applications, add members, record payments, view reports, manage trainers and settings

One person can have more than one role. For example, an owner who also trains clients sees the admin screens and the trainer screens in the same app.

How to use this guide

  • Read Getting Started first. It is the same for everyone.
  • Then jump to the part for your role. Each part walks through the screens in the order you will meet them.
  • Look in Gym Reference for hours, prices and contacts.
  • If something does not work, see Tips, Troubleshooting and FAQ near the end.

Getting Started

Everyone follows the same three steps: get the app, join (or be added), and sign in.

Step 1: Open the app

The gym will tell you how to get the app on your phone. It works on iPhone and Android. If your phone has large text turned on, the app adjusts, up to 1.3 times normal size.

Step 2: Join as a new member

New members join with an invite code. The gym texts it to your mobile phone.

  1. Open the app and go to Account.
  2. Choose the join option and enter your 8-digit invite code.
  3. Complete the six steps of the form:
    1. Services: what you are interested in (gym, training, classes).
    2. About you: your details. A photo is required.
    3. Your health: current health information.
    4. Health history: past injuries, conditions and medications.
    5. Goals: what you want to get out of training.
    6. Review and sign: read the health release and the liability waiver, type your full name to sign, and create your login.
  4. Wait for the gym to approve you. You will see a message that says the gym is reviewing your application until then.

Good to know

  • The invite code works once and expires after 7 days. Too many wrong tries makes you wait a few minutes before trying again.
  • Your login is an optional short name plus a password of at least 8 characters.
  • Your progress is saved on your phone for 7 days, so you can stop and come back. Your password is never saved.
  • People under 18 sign up in person with a parent or guardian.
  • Signed up another way, such as on paper? Ask the gym for a login code, then use Create your login on the Account screen.

Step 3: Sign in

Everyone signs in on the same screen: Account.

  1. Tap Account.
  2. Enter the sign-in name and password you created when you joined.
  3. Tap Sign in.

After you sign in, the app opens on the right page for your role, and the Account key turns into Settings.

What you see after signing in

A strip near the top reads "Signed in as ...". Staff see a green STAFF badge, and veterans see a small flag.

Moving around

  • Tap a key along the bottom to switch pages.
  • Long pages have a Back button at the bottom. Back returns you to the page you were just on.
  • Staff and trainers: you may be signed out when the app fully restarts. Members stay signed in.
  • To sign out, go to Settings (admins: More, then My settings) and tap Sign out.

Part 1: For Members

As a member you have four keys along the bottom: Home, Workouts, Plans and Settings.

Waiting for approval

After you submit your application, a gym admin reviews it. Until you are approved, signing in shows a message that the gym is still reviewing your application, and you can only see the guest pages (Hours, Plans, Chat, Account).

Home

Home is the first page you see. It shows:

  • Right now: whether the gym is currently staffed. Open gym is available 24 hours a day for registered members.
  • Call Bryan or Brandy: one tap to call the gym. These are calls only, no texts.
  • Directions: opens the gym location in your maps app.
  • This week: the week's hours and any special notes.
  • Your membership: the date you are paid through, the days left, and your last payment.
  • Your workouts: your last workout and your workout counts.

Workouts

The Workouts key shows the history of the workouts your trainer has logged for you, along with your counts. Tap a workout to see the exercises and sets.

Plans

The Plans key lists gym membership, personal training and class options and prices so you can see what is available. Payment is taken at the gym. You never pay inside the app.

Settings

Settings is where you manage your own account.

  • Add or change your photo.
  • See your sign-in name.
  • Sign out.

Scrolling banner

A banner across the top of some pages shows a message from the gym, such as a holiday closure or a special. It is just for information.

Part 2: For Trainers

Trainers have four keys along the bottom: Today, Clients, Exercises and Settings.

Today

Today is your starting page. It shows:

  • Workouts in progress, with a live clock.
  • Workouts waiting to upload. These were saved on your phone because there was no signal. They upload automatically when you are back online.
  • Your clients, each with their last workout. Clients you have not seen in 14 or more days are flagged.
  • A Watch line with anything to keep an eye on for a client.
  • Start or Continue buttons to open a session.

Clients

Tap Clients, then tap a client to open their details.

Goals What the client wants to achieve

Training-relevant health info Only the health details a trainer needs, for your own clients

Running a training session

The session screen is built to be used with one hand, in the gym, with a sweaty phone.

  1. Open a client and tap Start workout.
  2. At the top you see the client's name, the Watch line and the red 911 / Help button.
  3. The clock is pinned to the screen. Tap Start session when you begin. Use Pause and End session as needed.
  4. Pick an exercise from the exercise pills along the top. You work on one exercise at a time.
  5. Set the numbers with the big minus and plus steppers. Tap the number itself to type an exact value.
  6. Tap Log set. Your logged sets appear as chips. Tap a chip to edit or delete it.
  7. Tap Next exercise to move on, or Add an exercise to bring in a new one.
  8. Add a session note or an exercise note if you want one. Notes are links, so the keyboard only opens when you choose to type.
  9. Tap End session when you finish. The session is saved.

What the steppers change

Measure Each tap changes it by
Weight 5 lb
Reps 1 rep
Time 5 seconds
Distance 0.1

Which of these you see depends on the exercise: weight and reps, reps only, time, or distance and time.

Adding an exercise

Add an exercise opens a full screen picker. At the top you see From last time (what this client did before) and the client's favorites, so the usual exercises are one tap away. Filter by type to find others.

No signal? Keep going. Your sets are saved on your phone and upload later. Check Today to see anything still waiting.

911 / Help

If there is an emergency, tap the red 911 / Help button at the top of the session screen. It opens a full emergency screen with nothing else on it:

  • Call 911 button.
  • The gym's address, with the highway junction and landmarks, and Open in Maps.
  • The client's emergency contact and doctor.
  • The client's full health history.
  • Back to workout when it is over.

Exercises

The Exercises key is the gym's exercise list.

  • Use the type chips to filter.
  • Each exercise has a measure type: weight and reps, reps, time, or distance and time.
  • Use the For: picker at the top to choose a client and mark that client's favorite exercises with the star. Favorites show first when you add exercises in a session.
  • Remove an exercise you no longer use. You can Put back a removed one later.

Settings

On Settings you manage your own photo, your trainer profile and your sign-in name, and you can Sign out. If you are also a member, you have one photo for both roles.

Part 3: For Owners and Admins

Admins have four keys along the bottom: Today, Members, Payments and More. An admin who also trains clients has a fifth key, Clients. The More page holds everything else, grouped into folding sections: Every day, Training, Setup, My settings, App pages and Coming soon.

Today

Today is your daily dashboard:

  • Pending applications waiting for your review.
  • Payments overdue and due soon.
  • The Staffed switch.
  • Quick actions for the things you do most.

Admins also get a phone alert for each new application, if notifications are turned on.

Adding a new member

New members join with an invite code that you text to them. The app shows you exactly what the text will say before anything is sent.

  1. Go to Today (or Members) and choose New member.
  2. Enter the person's mobile number. Entering a name is optional.
  3. As you type, a message bubble shows They will get this text.
  4. Tap Make code. The invite code is created and appears in the message.
  5. Tap Send text from my phone. Your Messages app opens with the text ready to send.
  6. If you are in person, use Or read the code aloud instead.
  7. To invite someone else, tap Add another member. To fix a mistake in the number, tap Start over.

The code works once and expires after 7 days.

Reviewing an application

When a new member finishes the join form, it appears under Pending applications on Today.

  1. Tap an application. You review one applicant at a time.
  2. Check their details and photo.
  3. To read their health summary, tap to show it. Every time a health file is opened, the app logs it.
  4. Tap Approve, or Deny. Deny needs a second tap to confirm, so it cannot happen by accident.

Members

The Members key lists everyone. Open a member to:

  • Approve or deny an application.
  • Mark a member as former, or delete them.
  • Add a staff note.
  • Mark a veteran (shows a small flag next to their name).
  • Open the member file. Health information stays hidden until you choose to show it, and each view is logged.

Payments

Payments are always taken at the gym and recorded by hand. The app has no card processing.

The Payments key has three tabs:

Tab Shows
Due now Members who are overdue or about to be due
Never paid Members with no payment recorded yet
All Everyone

Set each member's typical payment. For each member, choose their plan (gym membership, personal training, 2-person training, or a class), the amount, and whether they usually pay by cash or card. The button reads Set payment until it is filled in. Use Edit to change it later.

Record a payment.

  1. Find the member and tap Record payment.
  2. Confirm the plan, amount and method.
  3. You have 5 seconds to Undo if you tapped by mistake.
  4. Optionally, send the member a text receipt. The app remembers your choice.

How due dates work

  • A paid-up member shows Paid through a date. They only move into Due now 7 days before that date. For week plans it is 2 days before. For day passes and single sessions, it is on the day.
  • Paying early? Use Edit on the member.
  • For now, training and class payments also move gym access forward one month.

Reports

Find Reports under More.

  • Money in: payments this month, last month or this year, split into cash and card, with totals by type.
  • Interests, classes, goals and age groups: what your members are asking for.
  • Veterans report.

Owner checklist

Every day

  • Review pending applications.
  • Check Payments, Due now, and text reminders as needed.
  • Set Staffed when staff arrive and leave.

Every week

  • Look at clients on Trainer Today who have not been seen in 14 or more days.
  • Check Security for repeated failed sign-ins or wrong invite codes.

Every month

  • Review Reports, Money in.
  • Update the scrolling banner for upcoming events or closures.

Gym Reference

The GYM is pay as you go: no contracts and no initiation fee. Payment is taken at the gym, never in the app. It is due on the same day each month, and there are no refunds.

Location

Your gym's address and directions are set up in the app by the owner, so members always see the right location.

Hours

Hours
Open gym 24 hours, every day, for registered members (keycode entry)
Staffed Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 6 PM
Training by appointment Mostly 8 AM to 12 PM and 3 PM to 6:30 PM. Sessions last 35 to 45 minutes.
Saturday and Sunday Open gym only

Contacts

Call Bryan or Brandy from the Home page in the app. Both are calls only, no texts. The gym is also on Facebook, linked from the Hours tab.

Memberships and training

Gym memberships are available by the day, week, month, 3 months, 6 months and year. Additional family members can join at a lower rate. Personal training and martial arts are offered in monthly session plans, with a 2-person rate for people in the same household who train on the same dates and times with the same trainer.

Current prices are shown on the Plans tab in the app, or ask the gym.

Privacy and Safety

The app handles health information, so it is built to share as little as possible.

  • Passwords are stored in scrambled (hashed) form. No one at the gym can read yours.
  • Only admins can open a member's full health file, and every time one is opened the app records it.
  • Trainers see only the training-relevant health details for their own clients.
  • There are no card payments in the app. You pay at the gym.
  • Notifications are optional.
  • Wrong sign-in attempts and wrong invite codes are logged so the owner can spot problems.

Tips, Troubleshooting and FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I pay in the app? No. Payment is taken at the gym.

Can I use the gym on weekends and at night? Yes. Open gym is 24 hours a day, every day, for registered members.

When is someone at the gym? Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 6 PM. The Home page shows whether the gym is staffed right now.

Can my husband, wife or a family member join at a discount? Yes. Gym membership has an "each additional person" price, and training has a 2-person rate for people in the same household who train together. See Gym Reference or the Plans tab.

Can a trainer see all my health information? No. Trainers see only what is relevant to training. Only admins can open the full file.

Is there a way to chat with a trainer in the app? Not yet. Call Bryan or Brandy.
